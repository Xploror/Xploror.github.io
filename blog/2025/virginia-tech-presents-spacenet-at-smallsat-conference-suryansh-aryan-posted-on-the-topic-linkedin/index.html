        🚀 Excited to share my latest publication in the International Journal of Satellite Communications and Networking!

Our article, “Advanced Fade Mitigation Techniques for Q/V Band SATCOM Systems”, explores how next-generation satellite communication systems can overcome one of their greatest challenges: atmospheric attenuation at high frequencies.

The Q/V band offers unprecedented bandwidth and is key to enabling terabit-per-second satellite networks. However, rain and other atmospheric effects can severely degrade signals. In this work, we introduce a simulation framework that integrates:

🌦️ Numerical weather predictions for realistic atmospheric modeling

📡 Smart Gateway Diversity (SGD), uplink power control, and adaptive coding/modulation

🔬 A detailed performance assessment of system behavior under realistic conditions

Our results show how SGD latency critically impacts feeder link availability and user data rates, highlighting the importance of fast and adaptive fade mitigation strategies for future broadband satellite services.

✨ This paper is part of a Special Issue on advances in satellite communications, to which we were invited following our presentation at the 29th Ka Conference 2024 (https://lnkd.in/dqv76sMB), where our work was selected among the best papers of the conference.
